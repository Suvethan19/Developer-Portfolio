About Me

Markham, Ontario • Remote / Hybrid / On-Site

Hello! I’m Suvethan. I recently completed my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Wilfrid Laurier University. My computer science journey has centered on understanding how data moves reliably between clients, distributed backends, and databases.

In my current role as a Junior Full Stack Developer at Voyzant Inc., I work directly with travel aggregators and Global Distribution Systems (GDS).
